Our recent classroom work

Updated October 7, 2026

Arts & Letters

We have started reading the chapter book The Birchbark House in Arts & Letters Module 1, Handed Down. We continue exploring the question, “How do communities sustain their culture?” As we read, we discuss characters and important events and use details from the text to explain our thinking.

Mathematics

We have been practicing adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators, mixed numbers, and improper fractions. Our Unit 1 review brings those skills together: finding common denominators, regrouping when needed, simplifying answers, and solving word problems. We use models and strategies to explain and check our work. Currently: We are on Lesson 3 of Math Expressions 2018 Unit 2, learning decimal place value. Find worked examples, tips, and homework videos.

Phonics & word study

In Lesson 3, we are focusing on syllable types and reading longer words. We break words into syllables and use vowel patterns to help us read unfamiliar words accurately and fluently.

MTSS reading

Our silent e reading work focuses on long vowels and vowel-consonant-e words. We read and spell words, compare pairs such as cap/cape and kit/kite, and explain how silent e changes the vowel sound.

Science: Shared Waters

We have been exploring watersheds and how water moves through the land into connected streams. We are looking at how pollution and human activity affect water quality and how we can protect our local watershed. This learning connects to our upcoming Brandywine Springs Park field trip.

Social studies: looking ahead

Our social studies work will explore economics, including how people make choices and use resources. We will apply these ideas in JA BizTown.

Our classroom community

We listen, help one another, own our choices, and celebrate growth. Every teammate belongs.
