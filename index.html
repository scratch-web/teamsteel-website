Family updateOctober 13, 2026

Hello Team Steel Families,

The permission slip for our October 26 field trip to Brandywine Springs Park is being sent home with the Shared Waters research consent form attached in your preferred home language. Please review, sign, and return the field trip permission slip at your earliest convenience. Please also review and complete the attached consent form; participation in the research is voluntary.

On Wednesday, October 14, I will send home your child’s reading comprehension test and Math Unit 1 assessment with the grades and feedback. Please review them with your child, sign both assessments, and return them.

I have also assigned two pages for homework tonight, Tuesday, October 13, in our new unit. Please encourage your child to complete the assignment carefully and return it tomorrow, Wednesday, October 14.

I believe consistent homework, strong effort, focus in class, open communication, and practice at home will help unlock each student’s success. Together, we can celebrate their strengths and support the skills they are still developing.

Thank you for your continued partnership!

Mr. Steel
